Why Rafiq?

A real problem many Deaf and hard-of-hearing people face when they need urgent help.

🧏

Communication barrier

Deaf and hard-of-hearing people can struggle with voice-based communication in emergencies, especially over phone calls.

🚨

Response speed

Emergencies need essential information delivered quickly and clearly, without misunderstanding or delay.

🤖

Assistive technology

AI and computer vision can support recognizing sign language and converting it into text and speech.

About Rafiq

Assistive technology for the Deaf and hard-of-hearing.

Project goal

Designing a digital tool that makes it easier for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people to request emergency services, by translating sign language into text or speech and sending essential information and location once the user agrees.

Potential uses

  • Requesting help in urgent situations.
  • Making communication easier between the user and an emergency operator.
  • Sending written details about the situation and location once confirmed by the user.
  • Use by organizations that serve Deaf and hard-of-hearing people.

Future work

  • Train an actual sign-recognition model (the next phase).
  • Recognize connected sentences instead of single signs.
  • Diversify training data and improve performance across lighting and backgrounds.
  • Support regional sign language variations.
  • Turn the operator's reply into text or a visual interface.
  • Run field tests with users from the target group.
